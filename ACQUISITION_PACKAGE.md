## Core architecture boundary

Convertible Cranium Synapse supplies proposals and bounded capability integration. Substrate A evaluates constitutional admissibility (“May we?”). Substrate B evaluates evidence grounding (“Is it so?”). Convertible Cranium Kernel is the canonical authority and convergence boundary. Miracle Meta Memory, Miracle Metacognitive Mapper, and Convertible Cranium COMA / Circuit Breaker remain separate supporting capabilities and do not independently grant execution authority.

## Evidence and claims rule

The health checks and architecture gate are useful local evidence, not independent certification of whole-system production readiness. The connected workspace has passed both boot-drive structural checks and the architecture contract gate for 30 registered boxes. Commander’s standard portable build, end-to-end UI, successful live-provider generation, clean-host ISO builds, software-license review, and legal chain of title remain explicit diligence gates.

**No claim of revenue, ARR, paying users, customer deployments, patents, certifications, or independently verified security should be made without separate documentary evidence.** Repository presence and commit history are not substitutes for proof of legal ownership.

This package is a diligence starting point, not an offer capable of acceptance, a valuation opinion, or legal advice. Transaction scope and terms require written agreement and counsel review.
