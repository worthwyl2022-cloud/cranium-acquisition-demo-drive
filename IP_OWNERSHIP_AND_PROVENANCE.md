## 3. Chain-of-title rule

The acquisition record must distinguish **technical provenance** from **legal ownership**.

Technical provenance may establish who created, committed, maintained, or published an artifact. Legal ownership must be established through applicable law and executed agreements, assignments, registrations, licenses, employment/contractor terms, and other controlling instruments.

All ownership language in this repository is therefore intentionally qualified pending attorney review.
