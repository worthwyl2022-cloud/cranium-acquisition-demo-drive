# Voice Reference Policy

Wyl Mathes may provide private voice recordings as development references for identity, pronunciation, timing, or future voice-system design. Raw recordings are personal reference material and are never part of the Commander distribution, boot image, Git history, or public evidence surface.

Commander may identify the existence of a private voice reference without embedding or serving the recording. Any future generated or recorded product voice must be separately authorized for distribution and must not be inferred from a private reference alone.
