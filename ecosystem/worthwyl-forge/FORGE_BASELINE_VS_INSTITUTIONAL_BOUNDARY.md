# Forge Baseline vs Institutional Forge Boundary

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the hard architectural boundary between the frozen Forge 1.0 baseline and the Forge Cognitive Institution evolutionary architecture.

## 2. Frozen Forge 1.0 Baseline

Forge 1.0 is frozen. The baseline retains its historical identity, implementation lineage, repository provenance, terminology, and verified evidence as they existed at the time of freeze.

Prohibited baseline mutations include renames performed solely to conform to the institutional architecture, relocation solely for architectural aesthetics, retroactive redesign, retroactive reclassification of historical evidence, rewriting historical claims to reflect later architecture, and silently replacing baseline implementations with institutional successors.

Security, correctness, interoperability, reliability, and evidence-driven fixes may be applied when they preserve baseline identity and authority semantics. Such changes remain explicitly versioned and documented.

## 3. Forge Cognitive Institution

The Forge Cognitive Institution is new architecture. It is a governed evolutionary extension of Convertible Cranium Software, not a mutation of Forge 1.0.

Its 41-document architecture is a master specification. Individual components acquire implementation status only through executable evidence.

## 4. Authority Boundary

Forge remains outside the Convertible Cranium core authority stack.

Forge governance may govern Forge's own product-local cognition, execution, memory, deliberation, and institutional processes. It does not become canonical Convertible Cranium authority by possessing a constitution, governance engines, institutional memory, or an internal identity.

Where canonical authority is required, Forge must use an explicit Convertible Cranium integration surface.

## 5. No Sideways Authority Movement

Authority does not move sideways from the Kernel into Forge, nor from Forge governance into the Kernel. Integration creates a governed interface, not an architectural merger.

The Convertible Cranium Kernel remains the canonical authority boundary.

## 6. Provenance Boundary

Historical Forge artifacts retain their original identity and lineage.

Institutional Forge artifacts identify originating version, predecessor artifact when applicable, implementation status, governing document, evidence references, integration status, and supersession state.

A later institutional artifact may supersede a prior artifact only through an explicit supersession record. Supersession does not erase history.

## 7. Integration Boundary

The institutional architecture may integrate with Convertible Cranium Kernel, Synapse, Commander, Chromium Edition, and Miracle Meta Memory through explicit governed contracts.

Integration is optional and additive. No integration may silently convert a Forge component into a core-stack component.

## 8. Boundary Decision Rule

When ambiguity exists, classify the artifact by executable authority path and provenance, not by naming, placement, UI presentation, or marketing language.

## 9. Final Boundary

Forge 1.0 is the frozen historical baseline. Forge Cognitive Institution is the versioned evolutionary architecture. They coexist. Neither may masquerade as the other.

**Boundary status: LOCKED.**
