## Top-Level Governance Statement

The Forge Cognitive Institution is an evolutionary architecture of Convertible Cranium Software. It does not modify, supersede, or retroactively redefine the frozen Forge 1.0 baseline. Its authority, capabilities, and integrations are established through explicit versioned extensions and remain subordinate to the Convertible Cranium authority hierarchy where core integration is invoked.

## Evidence Rule

No institutional component is promoted from specification to implemented status without executable code, an explicit contract, a test, integration evidence where applicable, and a registered verification path.

## Authority Rule

Forge may establish product-local governance and institutional decisions. It may not mint, impersonate, substitute for, or supersede canonical Convertible Cranium authority. Where core authority is required, the governed integration surface must explicitly invoke the Convertible Cranium authority path.

## Change Control

Changes to this governance binder or the institutional architecture require an explicit version, rationale, affected-document record, evidence status, and provenance link to the prior version. Changes must not silently mutate Forge 1.0 historical identity.

## Boundary Principle

Lock the baseline. Version the institution. Integrate explicitly. Preserve provenance. Keep authority in its canonical boundary.
