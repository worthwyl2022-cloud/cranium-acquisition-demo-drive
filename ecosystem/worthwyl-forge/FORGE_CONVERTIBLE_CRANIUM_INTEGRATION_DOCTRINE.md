# Forge-Convertible Cranium Integration Doctrine

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how the Forge Cognitive Institution may integrate with Convertible Cranium technology and the authority stack without becoming part of the core authority architecture.

## 2. Product Boundary

Forge is Convertible Cranium Software. Forge is independently deployable and independently licensable. Forge is not a core-stack component.

The institutional architecture does not redefine the identity, authority boundary, or provenance of the Convertible Cranium core stack.

## 3. Authority Hierarchy

The Convertible Cranium Kernel remains the canonical authority boundary.

Forge governance may make product-local governance decisions, enforce Forge constitutional requirements, and determine whether Forge is willing to proceed with work. Those decisions do not constitute canonical Convertible Cranium authority.

Where canonical authority is required, Forge must invoke the explicit governed Convertible Cranium authority path.

## 4. Integration Surfaces

### Convertible Cranium Kernel

Permitted surfaces include authority receipts, lineage verification, explicit authority evaluation, governed execution paths, and authority-bound evidence exchange.

Forge must not forge, reinterpret, or substitute Kernel receipts.

### Convertible Cranium Synapse

Permitted surfaces include risk contracts, intervention contracts, proposal/context exchange, and governed cognition interfaces. Synapse integration does not make Forge a Synapse component.

### Convertible Cranium Commander

Commander may serve as an operating surface for institutional workflows. Commander presentation or orchestration does not transfer canonical authority to Forge.

### Convertible Cranium Chromium Edition

Chromium Edition may provide a governed computing and execution environment for institutional Forge workflows. The execution environment remains distinct from Forge's institutional architecture.

### Miracle Meta Memory

Miracle Meta Memory may provide experiential continuity services to Forge. Memory imported from or exported to Forge remains evidence-bearing and provenance-bound. Memory does not become truth merely because Forge retained it.

## 5. Governed Interface Requirements

Every integration must define:
- input contract
- output contract
- identity and provenance
- version binding
- authority semantics
- failure behavior
- audit record
- verification path

Unspecified integration behavior must fail closed.

## 6. Non-Integration Guarantees

Forge does not:
- become part of the core authority stack
- inherit authority implicitly
- redefine the Kernel
- redefine Synapse
- redefine Commander
- redefine Chromium Edition
- alter Convertible Cranium naming
- mint canonical authority
- treat product-local governance as Kernel authority

## 7. Integration Evolution

New integrations require explicit version, integration contract, security review appropriate to scope, executable tests, provenance record, evidence status, and rollback or failure behavior.

Integration is additive, not absorptive.

## 8. Doctrine

**Forge may participate in the authority path. Forge does not become the authority path.**

**Integration status: LOCKED.**
